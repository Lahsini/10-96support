TRANSPARENCY & CONTROL

Privacy Policy.

A clear explanation of what 10-96 keeps on your device, when online services are used, and how you control your information.

Effective: November 5, 2026Contact: ten96dev@gmail.com

01 / OVERVIEW

What this policy covers

10-96 (also described in some technical materials as OfficerReference) is an independently developed iPhone reference and workflow utility. This policy explains how the app and its support website handle information.

10-96 does not require an account with the developer to use its core functions. Based on the release source reviewed for this policy, the app does not operate a developer-hosted account or cloud-sync service, and does not include advertising or cross-app tracking features. Many features work offline. Some features intentionally contact third-party services, and you can choose whether to use those features.

Important distinction: information saved on your iPhone is not the same as information sent to an online service or to support. We explain these separately below. The app is not an official NYPD, NYC or other government product.

02 / ON-DEVICE DATA

Information stored locally

The app can store information that you enter, create, scan, import or save, including:

  • Operational work: arrest drafts, defendant details, generated forms, saved summonses and photos, incident workspaces, imported paperwork, overtime entries and Officer Profile information.
  • Personal organization: flashcards and decks, vacation day selections, RDO/calendar preferences, Quick Access arrangement, theme settings and other preferences.
  • Reference and lookup caches: downloaded reference content, voice model files, public Officer Lookup responses and requested VIN decode results when cached.
  • Local recovery files: supported protected recovery copies and deleted-item recovery records.

These items are maintained on the device during normal operation; they are not automatically emailed to the developer or attached to Officer Lookup or VIN searches. Sensitive local stores use iOS file protection and feature-specific encryption where implemented. Protection can differ by feature and file type.

Documents may contain sensitive information entered by you. Enter, store and share information only as authorized by your organization and applicable rules. The app is not a substitute for an approved case-management system.

03 / NETWORK REQUESTS

Online services and third parties

When you enable a network-dependent action, the requested service may receive the information needed to complete it, as well as ordinary connection metadata such as your IP address. Third-party providers control their own processing, retention and logs under their policies.

04 / PERMISSIONS

Access requested from iOS

  • Location while using the app: to center maps and find nearby reference locations when you grant permission. The reviewed configuration does not request persistent “Always” location access.
  • Camera: for supported document, summons or VIN scanning when you start that action.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for voice search. The reviewed voice-search implementation requires supported on-device speech recognition. You can use typed search instead.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: for optional App Lock and protected deletion actions, as supported by your device.
  • Files and sharing interfaces: when you choose to import, save, back up or share a document.

You can decline or revoke applicable permissions in iPhone Settings. Some convenience features may be unavailable without a required permission; ordinary offline reading and reference functions do not depend on granting every permission.

05 / USER-CONTROLLED SHARING

Exports, backups and external storage

When you use the iOS share sheet, print, export, email, AirDrop, save to Files, or create a portable backup, the chosen destination may receive the selected data. 10-96 supports encrypted portable backups, but you are responsible for keeping the backup password safe and choosing an approved destination.

Data saved outside the app—such as an exported PDF, a file in iCloud Drive, an email attachment or a third-party storage provider—is governed by that destination's settings and policies. Removing a record inside 10-96 does not erase copies that you exported elsewhere. iOS device backups or sync features may also handle app data according to your Apple/device settings.

Before sharing or contacting support, remove confidential, personal, case-related and identifying content unless you are expressly authorized to disclose it.

06 / LIFECYCLE

Retention, recovery and deletion

Saved local records generally remain on your device until you delete them. Supported deleted records move to Recently Deleted, where they can be recovered or permanently removed. The app offers an optional setting to permanently purge eligible deleted items after 30 days. Recovery snapshots or rollback data may remain until separately cleaned up or replaced under the app's recovery controls.

Use Tools → Data → Backup & Restore for available recovery/deletion options. Permanent removal may require device authentication. Deleting the app generally removes its local app container from that device, but does not remove exports, iOS backups or copies saved in other services. Delete those copies separately.

Public-reference caches, downloaded models, temporary files and diagnostic information have feature-specific lifetimes and can be cleared or replaced through available controls, updates or device-management actions.

07 / PROTECTION

Security and practical limitations

10-96 uses platform protections and local safeguards, including supported iOS data protection, optional device authentication and protected backup functionality. No device, encryption implementation or transfer method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep iOS updated, use a strong device passcode, secure backups and avoid sharing sensitive records through unapproved channels.

READ voice playback and the supported offline voice model are designed to operate on-device, including while your phone is locked where supported. As with any accessibility or audio feature, be mindful of reading confidential material aloud where others can hear it.

08 / SUPPORT WEBSITE

Information you send to support

If you contact ten96dev@gmail.com, we receive what you voluntarily include, such as your email address, app version, device/OS details, issue description and attachments. We use that information to respond, investigate and improve support, and retain correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, protect against misuse and meet any applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete support correspondence we control, subject to lawful exceptions.

This support website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. The pages supplied with this policy do not install analytics, ad pixels or tracking cookies and do not provide a web form. GitHub and your browser/network provider may nevertheless handle ordinary website-access data under their own policies. If you click an email link, your chosen email service handles the message.

We do not sell support correspondence to advertisers. Please do not send passwords, unredacted arrest paperwork, confidential records or sensitive case details to support.

09 / CONTROLS

Your choices, access and requests

  • Use offline features without triggering optional VIN, Officer Lookup or external web requests.
  • Change camera, location, microphone, speech recognition and authentication access using iOS Settings.
  • Review, correct, export or delete locally managed records using the applicable in-app controls.
  • Delete files and backups separately from any destination outside the app.
  • Contact support to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you deliberately emailed us.

Because 10-96 does not maintain a developer-hosted account of your local records in the reviewed version, support generally cannot access, recover, correct or erase records that exist only on your device. Additional rights may apply under the laws where you live.
