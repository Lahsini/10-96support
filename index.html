A few common questions, answered.
Still need a hand? Email support.

How do I contact support?

Email ten96dev@gmail.com with your question. Include your app version, iPhone model, and iOS version if you are reporting a technical issue.

What should I do if the app is not responding?

Close and reopen 10-96, then check for an app update in the App Store. If the issue continues, restart your iPhone and contact support with the steps that triggered it. Before deleting or reinstalling the app, make sure any important saved information is backed up.

How do I report incorrect reference information?

Send the feature name, the entry or section involved, and a description of the correction. If available, include a link to the official source. Always verify operational decisions against current official sources and applicable department guidance.

What should I include in a bug report?

Describe what you were doing, what you expected, and what actually happened. Include your app version, iPhone model, and iOS version. A screenshot can help, but remove personal, case-related, or confidential information first.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

Read the 10-96 Privacy Policy for details about on-device records, optional online lookups, permissions, backups, deletion and support requests.

Who owns the guides and reference materials?

10-96 is independent. Original guides, public datasets, third-party marks, and licensed voice software remain subject to their respective owners and license terms. See Content Rights & Disclaimers.

Can I request a new feature?

Yes. Email your idea and explain the task it would help you complete. Suggestions help inform future improvements, though specific features and release dates are not guaranteed.

The email button does not open. What can I do?

The contact links open your device’s email app. If no email app is configured, open your preferred email service and send a message directly to ten96dev@gmail.com.
